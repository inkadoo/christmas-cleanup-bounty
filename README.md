# Christmas Cleanup

Clock in at the check-in lodge. Pick up ten elf ragdolls across the Great Hall, courtyard, wrapping workshop and kitchen. Carry each one to the Great Hall and press F at the fireplace prompt to throw it in. You have three minutes, and one player can run a shift at a time. Roblox proximity prompts support keyboard, touch and gamepad.

Pick up a mop at the Great Hall rack with E. Equip it and click or tap near a blood splatter to clean it. After eight successful strokes, rinse it at the bucket with R (Y on a gamepad). Specific action prompts appear on touch devices. Mopping is optional and does not change the elf count.

Open the accompanying Roblox place and choose Play. The server builds the map at runtime. Build from source with `rojo build -o "Christmas Cleanup - Lodge.rbxlx"`.

All geometry and effects were made from Roblox primitives and built-in objects. The game depicts stylized blood and fantasy violence. Disclose both in the Roblox content ratings questionnaire before release.
